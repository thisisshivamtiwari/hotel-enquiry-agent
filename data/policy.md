# Fernhollow House, Lowmere, Cumbria: guest information

A 17-room country house hotel, 2 miles from Lowmere station. Book online at https://book.fernhollow-hotel.example/
(the best rate is always on our own site).

## Rooms
- Classic Double (sleeps 2), Garden King (sleeps 2; dog-friendly; a cot fits), Family Suite (sleeps 4: up to 3 adults, or
  2 adults and 2 children; a cot or an extra bed fits), Accessible Double (sleeps 2; ground floor, step-free from the car
  park, roll-in shower, grab rails).
- Rates are per room per night, room only, and change with the date. Single guests pay the room rate.
- We have one rate: flexible. Nothing is charged at booking; a card guarantees the room and we take payment on arrival.

## Arrival and departure
- Check-in 3pm to 10pm. Arriving after 10pm? Tell us when you book or message us; we send a key-safe code on the day.
- Check-out by 11am. Late check-out until 1pm costs £25, asked for on the day, if the room is free.
- Early check-in can't be guaranteed. We store luggage free from 9am.

## Food
- Breakfast 7:30 to 10am: £14 per adult, £7 per child aged 4 to 12, free under 4. Vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free
  breakfasts on request.
- The restaurant serves dinner Wednesday to Sunday, 6pm to 9pm; booking advised. No dinner on Monday or Tuesday.

## Dogs, children, extras
- Dogs: Garden King rooms only, one dog per room (or two small dogs), £20 per stay. Dogs are welcome in the bar and lounge,
  not the restaurant.
- Cots are free in Garden King rooms and Family Suites; ask when you book. Extra beds only in Family Suites.
- Parking: 14 free spaces for guests, first come, first served; spaces can't be reserved. 2 EV chargers, free for guests.
- No lift: the first floor is reached by stairs. Guests who can't use stairs should book an Accessible Double.
- No pool, gym or spa.

## Cancellation
- Free cancellation until 2pm two days before arrival. After that, or if you don't arrive, we charge the first night.
- Cancel or change a booking with the link in your confirmation email, or contact reservations.

## Who to contact
- Reservations (bookings, changes, cancellations): reservations@fernhollow-hotel.example, 01632 960117.
- Events (5 or more rooms, weddings, parties, meetings, private dining, exclusive use): events@fernhollow-hotel.example.
  Group and event prices come only from the events team.
- Guest relations (complaints, charges, refunds): care@fernhollow-hotel.example; we reply within one working day.
- During your stay: the duty manager on 01632 960118, any time.
